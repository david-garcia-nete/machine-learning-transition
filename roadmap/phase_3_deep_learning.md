# Phase 3: Deep Learning and PyTorch

## Purpose

Develop hands-on fluency with neural networks and PyTorch. The goal is to understand training mechanics, debugging strategies, and the practical tradeoffs behind deep learning systems.

## Topics

- Tensors and automatic differentiation
- Neural network modules and training loops
- Optimizers, learning rates, and loss functions
- Regularization, initialization, normalization, and overfitting
- Embeddings, convolutional networks, and sequence modeling
