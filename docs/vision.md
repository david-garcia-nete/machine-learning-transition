# Vision

## Career Direction

The purpose of this transition is to become a machine learning engineer who can combine strong software engineering discipline with practical model development, evaluation, and deployment judgment.

The target profile is not a research-only role and not a purely application-layer AI integration role. The goal is to operate across the full applied ML lifecycle:

- Understand the problem and data-generating process.
- Choose useful baselines before complex models.
- Train and evaluate models with appropriate metrics.
- Build reproducible experiments and reliable pipelines.
- Deploy models behind maintainable systems.
- Monitor performance, failures, drift, and business impact.

## Long-Term Operating Principles

- Build systems that make model behavior observable.
- Prefer measurable improvement over novelty.
- Understand enough math to debug and reason, not just call APIs.
- Use PyTorch and modern tooling to understand deep learning directly.
- Treat data quality, evaluation, and iteration as first-class engineering concerns.

## Desired Evidence of Progress

- [ ] A completed 12-week sprint with written weekly reviews.
- [ ] At least one portfolio project with a clear baseline, evaluation method, and documented tradeoffs.
- [ ] Practical fluency with PyTorch training loops.
- [ ] Working understanding of transformer architecture and LLM application patterns.
- [ ] MLOps notes covering deployment, monitoring, experiment tracking, and reproducibility.
